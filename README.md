<h2> What is Trap And or Skeet? </h2>
<p>Trap shooting and Skeet shooting are popular shotgun sports where shooters aim at flying clay targets, often called “clay pigeons.” Both sports test accuracy, focus, timing, and hand-eye coordination, but they are set up differently.

In trap shooting, shooters stand in a line behind a trap house that throws targets away from them. The targets can move left, right, or straight ahead at different angles, making each shot unpredictable. Shooters usually rotate through five stations and take turns shooting at one target at a time. Trap focuses on quick reactions and aiming at targets moving away at high speed.

In skeet shooting, shooters move through eight stations arranged in a half-circle between a high house and a low house. Targets are launched from both houses and cross through the middle of the field. Sometimes shooters fire at a single target, and other times they shoot two targets at once called a “double.” Skeet requires smooth movement, timing, and the ability to track targets coming from different directions.
